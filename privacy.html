Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is a static informational website for a charitable foundation in formation. We collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. The giving waitlist form does not transmit data anywhere until giving opens; nothing you type leaves your browser.

Hosting

The site is served by our hosting provider, which may log standard technical request data (IP address, user agent) to operate and secure the service.

When giving opens

Before any form transmits data, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected, why, and how donor information is protected. Donor information will never be sold or shared for marketing.
